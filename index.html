The working day

Lunch is taken out of any day long enough to have had one. Dinner is taken out only of a night or longer — someone staying late on overtime is not given dinner, so nothing is deducted from them for it. A day is 8.5 hours on the floor for 8 paid, a day plus a night is 15 hours for 14, and a double night is 19 hours for 18.

Keep this at 30 so a day is worth the same in every month. Sundays and declared closures are paid rest days.

Night pay ladder

A full night adds one day's pay. A double night adds two. Anything short of a full night is paid proportionally.

A Sunday is a shorter day that still pays a full day. "Work day starts at" keeps an overnight shift attached to the day it began — leave it earlier than any shift start.

Backup

Downloads everything — settings, roster, every punch, closures and corrections — as one file. Keep a copy somewhere other than this phone. Do this monthly.

Restore

Reads a backup file back in. Settings, roster and closures are replaced. Attendance history is added separately and marked as restored, because a past punch can never be written back as if it were live — that is what keeps your records trustworthy.
